BLACKJACK STORY MODE - Standalone Edition
==========================================

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS:
- Windows 10 or higher (64-bit) / Linux / macOS
- 100 MB free disk space
- Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari)
- Internet connection NOT required (runs locally)

QUICK START:
1. Double-click the BlackjackStoryMode executable
2. Wait for the browser to open automatically (1-2 seconds)
3. Start playing!

HOW TO PLAY:
============

GAME OBJECTIVE:
- Start with $50 and try to reach $1,000,000
- Survive through story events and casino nights
- Make smart choices to maximize your chances

GAMEPLAY FLOW:
1. Casino Night - Play 3 rounds of blackjack (4 with Golden Watch)
2. End of Day - Review your stats and see your progress
3. Morning - Experience story events, meet NPCs
4. Afternoon - Visit shops, buy items, or return to casino
5. Repeat the cycle until you win or lose everything

STORY SYSTEM:
- 47 unique story events based on your wealth level
- Events change as you progress from Poor → Nearly There
- Choices affect your health, money, and available options
- Random encounters keep each playthrough unique

SHOPS & ITEMS:
- Doctor's Office (always available) - Health treatments
- Witch's Hut (unlocks at $1,000+) - Potions and magical items
- Repair Shops - Fix broken items (unlock by meeting mechanics)
- Auto Shops - Car maintenance services
- Marvin's Store - General goods (unlock by finding Map)

SPECIAL ITEMS:
- Golden Watch: Grants +1 casino round per night
- Health Indicator: Shows your exact health status
- Delight Indicator: Reveals happiness levels
- Flask of No Bust: Prevents blackjack busts
- And more...

GAME FEATURES:
==============
✓ Authentic blackjack gameplay with proper rules
✓ Forced story progression (no skipping content)
✓ Minimalist, creepy retro aesthetic
✓ Hidden mechanics for mystery and discovery
✓ Item durability system
✓ NPC encounter system
✓ Rank-based progression (6 wealth tiers)
✓ Auto-save within gaming session

CONTROLS:
=========
- Mouse: Click buttons to make choices
- Betting: Use quick bet buttons or custom amount
- Casino: Hit, Stand, or Deal buttons
- Story: Click "Continue" to progress through events
- Shops: Click items to purchase

TIPS FOR SUCCESS:
=================
1. Manage your bankroll carefully in the casino
2. Don't bet everything on one hand
3. Visit the Doctor when health is low
4. Buy items from shops to unlock bonuses
5. Meet the mechanics early to unlock repair services
6. Find the Map to access Marvin's store
7. Save money for expensive but valuable items
8. Watch for special events at higher wealth ranks

TROUBLESHOOTING:
================

Browser doesn't open automatically:
→ Manually navigate to http://127.0.0.1:5000/

Port 5000 already in use:
→ Close other applications using that port
→ Or edit the port in the executable configuration

Firewall warning:
→ Allow the application through your firewall
→ The app only listens on localhost (127.0.0.1)

Game freezes or crashes:
→ Refresh the browser page
→ Your progress within the session will be lost
→ Restart the executable to begin a new game

Black screen in browser:
→ Wait a few seconds for the server to fully start
→ Try refreshing the page
→ Check console window for error messages

TECHNICAL DETAILS:
==================
- Runs a local Flask web server on your computer
- All game data stored in temporary session (not saved to disk)
- No internet connection required
- No data collection or external connections
- Safe to close by closing browser and console window

TO EXIT THE GAME:
=================
1. Close the browser tab
2. Close the console/terminal window
   - Or press Ctrl+C in the console window
